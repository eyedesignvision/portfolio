About

A little more about how I work

I'm the kind of designer who'd rather redo something three times than ship a version I don't actually believe in, ask anyone who's watched me rebuild a "finished" project after one honest conversation with a real client.

I'm curious well outside of design, currently working toward a second Master's alongside my design practice, and I've always been drawn to the quiet mathematics behind things that feel timeless, proportion, structure, systems that hold up under real use rather than just looking good in a mockup.

With clients, I lead with questions before I lead with screens. I'd rather sit with an uncomfortable piece of feedback than protect a design I already like the look of.
